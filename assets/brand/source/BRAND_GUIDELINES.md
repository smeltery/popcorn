# Popcorn — Brand Kit v1.0

Project: https://github.com/smeltery/popcorn

## Brand positioning
Popcorn is a developer tool for recording, replaying, streaming, hosting, and rendering terminal sessions. Its recording format is `.pop`.

**Proposed tagline:** Make your terminal sessions pop.

**Voice:** Friendly, direct, playful but technically precise. Prefer short active sentences, clear CLI examples, and avoid exaggerated product claims.

## Typography
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** headings, UI and wordmarks (proposed; no font files included).
- **JetBrains Mono:** commands, code snippets, and technical metadata.

## Logo system
- Primary: mascot with laptop and red-white cinema tub.
- App/avatar: mascot square crop, keeping the face visible.
- Wordmark: lowercase `popcorn` in a rounded heavy face; create as separate vector artwork before production.
- Monochrome: single-color simplified silhouette for constrained contexts; not yet supplied.

## Usage rules
- Preserve proportions and leave at least 10% clear space around the mascot.
- On dark backgrounds use the transparent mascot asset.
- For small icons, omit floating decorative popcorn and rays; use a simplified face/tub mark.
- Do not stretch, recolor individual mascot elements, or place on busy backgrounds without contrast.
- The concept poster is a visual mockup, not a production-ready source for individual logos or UI screens.

## Suggested copy
**Short:** Record, replay, and share terminal sessions.

**Long:** Popcorn helps you capture terminal sessions as `.pop` files, replay them in your terminal or browser, stream live sessions, and render recordings to GIFs.

**Example commands:**
```sh
popcorn rec demo.pop
popcorn play demo.pop
```
