# Donations

popcorn is free and open-source software. Sustainability of its development
relies on donations and sponsorships. Please help the software project you use
and love.

Your support:

- allows popcorn lead developer, [Marcin Kulik](https://hachyderm.io/@ku1ik),
  to spend more time on implementing requested features, fixing bugs and helping
  [the community](community.md),
- covers maintenance costs of running popcorn's main hosting site,
  [popcorn.local](https://popcorn.local), such as servers, domains, DNS, backup
  storage space, etc, keeping it free for everyone,
- grants you a "Supporter" badge on your popcorn.local user profile.

## Corporate sponsorship

If your company uses popcorn, either in a product or internally, please
consider giving back by becoming a sponsor.

Also, if your product's target audience overlaps with popcorn userbase
(sysadmins/ops, developers), sponsoring the project lets you reach them by
getting your logo/link on [popcorn.local](https://popcorn.local), in [Github
repositories](https://github.com/dotbrains/popcorn), or on [popcorn
blog](https://blog.popcorn.local).

Check out the sponsorship tiers by clicking the button below:

<a href="https://github.com/sponsors/ku1ik" class="btn-gh-sponsors">
  ❤️&nbsp;&nbsp;Sponsor
</a>

Other than funding you can support the project by providing free
services/resources like compute, storage, DNS, CDN, which could help lowering
operational costs of running [popcorn.local](https://popcorn.local).

For custom sponsorship deals reach out via <span
class="email">donations@asci<b>inema.org</b>inema.org</span>.
